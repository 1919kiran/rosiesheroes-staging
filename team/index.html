Meet the Team

Rosie’s Heroes is led entirely by volunteers who believe every dog deserves love and safety. Meet the board members and volunteers turning that belief into action, and the dogs who hold a special place in their hearts.

Priyanka Rao

President

After a decade of volunteering and fostering dogs, Priyanka founded Rosie’s Heroes to give more dogs a chance at safety, healing, and love.

Why Rosie’s Heroes

As a child, Priyanka loved Rosie but couldn’t give her the safety and care she deserved. Rosie’s Heroes is her promise to do for dogs like Rosie what she couldn’t do then. After fostering 30+ dogs, she saw that rescue alone could never keep pace with the suffering. She remains committed to every individual dog while tackling the root causes through spay/neuter and empowering communities with the resources to help dogs themselves—creating lasting change beyond a single rescue.

Archana Ramamoorthy

Treasurer

Archana brings her leadership experience and a lifelong connection to India’s street dogs to her role as Treasurer. She helps guide the financial stewardship of Rosie’s Heroes, keeping resources focused on the dogs and communities they serve.

Why Rosie’s Heroes

Growing up in Chennai, Archana knew street dogs as familiar neighborhood guardians. Seeing dogs like them face hunger, injury, and overpopulation today makes this mission deeply personal. Through Rosie’s Heroes, she wants to help give those neighborhood companions the care and safety they deserve.

Sonia Dong

Secretary

As Secretary, Sonia helps keep the work of Rosie’s Heroes organized and moving forward—from documenting board decisions and maintaining records to helping the team follow through on its commitments. She brings that same care to a mission inspired by Soleil, a beloved source of comfort and companionship for her family.

Why Rosie’s Heroes

Soleil has brought so much comfort and companionship to Sonia and her family. Sonia wants more dogs to know the life Soleil has: loved, safe, and surrounded by people who cherish them. Rosie’s Heroes gives her a way to help make that possible.

Volunteers with Heart

Behind every second chance are people giving their time, talent, and love. Meet the volunteers making rescue possible—and discover what drew them here.

Shreya Singh

Why I care: Helping plan events is my way of bringing people together for dogs who need them. I love turning a shared love for animals into a gathering that inspires people to help.

Sai Kiran Mudupu

Why I care: Walking dogs at the shelter in my free time reminds me how much a little attention and kindness can mean. Helping with the Rosie’s Heroes website is another way I can support dogs beyond those walks.

Avani Ashok

Why I care: From walking dogs at the shelter to helping with the Rosie’s Heroes website, I love finding different ways to help. Time spent with the dogs makes the mission feel personal, and the website helps bring more people into it.
