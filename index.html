Why "Rosie's Heroes"?

Rosie's Heroes began with a loving Indian street dog named Rosie.

Every day, she would come to check on a young child she had quietly chosen to protect and walk alongside to the local biscuit shop. The family moved away to the U.S. and Rosie stopped coming to that street. Neighbors assumed the worst.

A few years later, the family returned and the very next day, Rosie was back — still watching, still loyal, still remembering the child she chose to adopt. Without missing a beat, she walked her child to the same biscuit shop, remembering the path they would take and keeping them safe from other dogs and people in the street.

But Rosie had nothing. No medical care. No reliable food. No safety. No guarantee she would live to see the next day.

𐂯

And she is not alone...

Across India, 60–70 million street dogs

live the same reality—offering love and trust in return for hunger, disease, and, too often, cruelty. Many are run over by cars, beaten, abused, and left to suffer for no fault of their own. A fear of dog bites fuels blanket misconceptions about street dogs and they are left to suffer their fate instead of being treated humanely as living beings.

When we first started helping street dogs, we thought the answer was simple: rescue more, feed more, save more. But the reality on the ground was heavier. Puppies kept being born. Communities were afraid, leading to cruelty, abuse, and even mass culling. Bite incidents occurred due to overpopulation and the need to survive. And families were still losing children to rabies from unvaccinated dog bites.

If we truly care about dogs and people, we can't just treat symptoms. We have to stabilize populations through sterilization and vaccination at scale. Rosie's Heroes grew from that realization.

Rosie's Heroes exists to replace fear with care and suffering with safety.

Vision

Rosie's Heroes is not just focused on individual rescue but on reducing suffering at scale for dogs and people. We believe that dogs and people deserve to live in a compassionate world where every neighborhood is safe and animals are treated humanely. We partner locally to sterilize and vaccinate dogs at scale, reducing bite incidents, lowering public health costs, and stabilizing communities.

Broken to Brave

Our mission is to transform dogs who have faced hardship and trauma into brave heroes who bring joy, love, and companionship to their adopted families. Every dog deserves care, love and safety.

Compassion into action

Our Mission

Millions of street dogs suffer every day — not because people don’t care, but because the scale of the crisis overwhelms individual rescuers. We focus our energy where it creates the most lasting change: preventing suffering at its source and giving local communities the tools to help themselves.

✦
01

Trauma Care

Prevention takes time, and dogs are suffering right now. We fund urgent surgeries and medical treatment for injured and critically ill street dogs — because every life in front of us matters as much as the thousands we aim to protect tomorrow.

02

Spay & Neuter

One surgery prevents countless future puppies from being born into hunger, disease, and danger. Sterilization is the single most humane and effective way to shrink street-dog populations — and sustained coverage is what finally collapses rabies transmission in a community.

03

Infrastructure

A rescue can only do what its tools allow. We fund ambulances, ICUs, X-ray machines, and shelter sheds so local teams can treat more animals, faster, for years to come. Infrastructure turns one-time donations into permanent capacity — empowering communities to help themselves.

Small acts. Lasting change.

Scottie

As a young puppy on the streets of Hyderabad, Scottie unfortunately lost one eye when she was run over by a car. With her disability, she received no adoption interest for over a year in India. I came across her profile when she was made available for adoption in the Bay Area and Scottie popped right out of the screen at me—her face conveying this beautiful joy and curiosity. When we met, I was instantly endeared to her sweet temperament and playful nature. She has been a bright light in my life ever since, and I am so grateful she found her way to me.

We go on walks and to the park every day. She is always up for a new adventure and makes friends everywhere she goes. I am honored to be able to introduce her as an Indie dog and share her story in my community. 🐾

📍Hyderabad → Bay Area💛One-Eyed Wonder🌟Adventure Seeker

— Marianne
